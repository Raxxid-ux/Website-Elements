How I Think

Clarity begins long before pixels move.

Good interfaces feel effortless because the underlying cognitive work has already been resolved. My workflow moves systematically from human context to verifiable usability.

Core Intersection
Psychology × Technology × Human Experience
01

Understand: Human context and psychology

Mental Models & Friction

Observing how people actually interpret information under stress, time constraints, or unfamiliarity—identifying cognitive load, emotional hesitation, and unspoken assumptions before defining features.

02

Investigate: Systemic problem framing

Root Cause & Constraints

Looking beyond isolated screen requests to map workflows, edge cases, technical constraints, and business incentives so the team solves the underlying systemic problem rather than a symptom.

03

Structure: Information architecture and logic

Hierarchy & Progressive Disclosure

Organizing complex state, navigation trees, and decision pathways so users see only what matters at each moment while keeping deeper control predictably reachable.

04

Design: Interaction, visual communication, and ergonomics

Typography, Space & Feedback

Crafting calm, high-contrast interfaces with deliberate typographic hierarchy, tactile micro-feedback, and reusable design tokens that scale across product teams.

05

Refine: Usability evaluation and iteration

Validation & Edge Cases

Testing prototypes with real users, observing where comprehension slows down, and iterating closely with engineers until the live product honors the intent.

About
